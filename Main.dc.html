The premise

The surface is healing. You just have to keep working.

After a collapse no one inside remembers clearly, the survivors live in the Apiary: a hexagonal complex hundreds of meters underground. Every resident works for the Ascent Project. Hit your quotas, and humanity returns to the surface.

An AI called the Architect sets the targets, grades every resident, and speaks through a vast screen called the Wall, which shows the surface healing a little more each year.

None of it is real. Above ground, a small group called the Keepers lives on for centuries, sustained by organs, blood and marrow taken from below. The residents were never workers. They are livestock with job titles.

The story · Spoilers ahead

Three degrees of awakening.

The Stone

  • Ezra is three cycles from Perfect Ashlar and proud of it.
  • His mentor Oriel predicts the exact date of the next setback. He laughs it off.
  • The setback hits as she said. Oriel is flagged and taken to the Infirmary.
  • Fixing a vent, Ezra finds Smoke canisters tagged with harvest batch numbers. One carries his ID.

The Wall

  • Ezra's own forecasts come true every time. The Architect marks them as noise. He has become a Cassandra.
  • Behind the Wall: 40 years of looped footage. The same three frames of birds, repeating.
  • He finds Oriel alive in the Extractor, a donor in rotation.
  • He hacks his score to Perfect Ashlar and is summoned upward for the Final Degree.

Outside the Cave

  • The surface is green. The Keepers live in a quiet, aging estate and explain the colony in spreadsheet terms.
  • They depend on the Architect and no longer understand it. Nobody is checking its work.
  • Ezra goes back down, shuts off the Smoke and tells everyone the truth.
  • Most don't believe him. The Wall still says 60% restored.
